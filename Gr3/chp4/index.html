👤 Personal Pronouns

Personal pronouns take the place of specific people, places, or things.

Person Singular (1) Plural (More than 1)
1st Person (Speaker) I, me we, us
2nd Person (Listener) you you
3rd Person (Others) he, him, she, her, it they, them

🎒 Possessive Pronouns & Adjectives

Possessive pronouns show ownership (who owns or has something).

Possessive Pronouns:
mine yours his hers ours theirs

Example: "That backpack is mine."

Possessive Words before Nouns:
my your his her its our their

Example: "This is my backpack."
